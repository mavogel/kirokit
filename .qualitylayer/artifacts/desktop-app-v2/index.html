Desktop app

What the person does: pick their team, install its agents into Kiro, keep them in sync, and give owners feedback, without typing commands.

What stays and changes: this draws the app as it ships today, every page and its main states, as the baseline for the next design change. Nothing in it is new yet; the states it leaves out are listed below.

App state

Switch pages with the app's own sidebar. Install, Cancel, Collapse, the terminal tabs and the Version list respond; nothing runs, is sent or is saved.

What this shows

Drawn from the app's source

  • Layout, sidebar, dock and dialogs: desktop/frontend/index.html
  • Colours, type and spacing: the tokens in src/style.css, light and dark
  • Cards, tables, badges and copy: src/pages/*.js and src/dialogs.js
  • Teams, agents, skills and pins: teams/*/manifest.yaml, the agent templates and env/mcp-versions.yaml

What is simulated

  • Sidebar, Collapse, terminal tabs, Hide/Show terminal, the resize handle (arrow keys) and both dialogs respond.
  • Picking another Version turns Update into “Switch to … and update”, as in the app.
  • No command runs, no folder opens, nothing is sent or saved; Save and Clone only show what the app would say.

Sample values and gaps

  • Sample: the install record (14 files, 18 Oct 2026), the branch and tag names, the commit, the /Users/you paths and the command output in the terminal.
  • The dock starts closed, as in the app; it opens on the kirokit tab when a command runs.
  • Not drawn: a catalog read error; the Teams notice for no release or a failed clone; a failed clone or switch; Settings field errors and the shell note; the busy labels Cloning…, Switching… and Exporting…; the No owner cell; the branch and pinned-tag badges; Windows and Linux title bars.
  • The app is desktop-only: at phone width the window scrolls sideways in its frame.
